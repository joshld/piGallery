Bins and where they sit

Icons that share a spot are shown together. Top left covers the clock, top right covers the weather, and the bottom can cover a caption.

When they stay on screen

They stay on the photos the whole time, from that many days before until the end of collection day. 2 means Wednesday and Thursday when collection is Friday, and Friday itself.

How often to update

1 day means daily, at this Brisbane time. The frame keeps the last dates if the update fails.
